# 原文（真题邮件，书 P27）
Hi Anna,
I've just been to the museum in our city. That was my first visit, believe it or {27}! I wanted to collect some information for our class history project. We have to hand it {28} soon, don't we?
I went to the Ancient History section, {29} the museum keeps all its ancient Egyptian stuff. It was really interesting! There were some amazing statues of various animals, so I drew some pictures of them and then {30} some research about them online when I got home.
I've still got some work to do on my project, so I'll need to go back to the museum again some time soon. In fact, {31} don't we go together? I don't think you've been there before, {32} you? I'm sure you'll find something that you could use for your project.
See you soon!
Sally

# 训练部分（书 P26，答案见 P187）
① 大意三题：text type b（an article）/ writer's purpose a（to encourage young people to study abroad）/ topic c（teenagers studying abroad）
② 填空（一空一词）：1 most / 2 addition / 3 to / 4 their
   （in addition to 除…之外；get used to 习惯于；on their own 靠他们自己）
③ 连接词：1 as / 2 long / 3 apart / 4 case / 5 order / 6 instead
   （as 因为；as long as 只要；apart from 除了…；in case 以防万一；in order to 为了；instead of 而不是）
④ 改错：1 that→than / 2 unless→if / 3 wherever→whenever / 4 too→also /
   5 because→because of / 6 Despite→Although

# 笔记（手写批注，整理进「析 → 笔记（书上批注）」）
P26（训练页）:
  ③-1 as 由于，因为 / ③-2 as long as 只要 / ③-3 apart from 除了… /
  ③-4 in case 以防万一；in fact 事实上 / ③-5 in order to do sth 为了去做某事 /
  ③-6 instead of 代替，作为替代；② 的四个空（most/addition/to/their）写的就是答案
P27（真题页）:
  believe it or not 信不信由你 / collect 收集 / hand in 提交，上交 /
  Ancient History section 远古的历史区 / ancient Egyptian stuff 古埃及的东西 /
  statues 雕像 / various 各种各样的 / research 研究 / some time 某个时间 /
  空里填的答案：27 not / 28 in / 29 where / 30 did / 31 why / 32 have

# 真题（书 P27，答案见 P187 官方解析）
27 not（believe it or not 固定短语）
28 in（hand in 动词短语；代词放中间：hand it in）
29 where（逗号 + 非限制性定语从句；先行词是地点 section）
30 did（do some research 做研究；与 drew 并列，过去时）
31 why（why don't we…? 提建议）
32 have（反意疑问句：前句否定 → 尾问肯定；助动词抄 've）
